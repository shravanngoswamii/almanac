Welcome. This page lives at `docs/index.md`, and it is the entry point for your
documentation.

## Editing this site

Two files control almost everything:

- `astro.config.mjs` holds the Almanac config: title, social links, sidebar,
  themes, and which features are on.
- `docs/` holds your Markdown. Add a file, and the route exists.
